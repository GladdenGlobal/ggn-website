Gladden Global Network is a veteran-owned global concierge real estate company headquartered in Maryland. Built to be the platform that connects the dots, educates every participant, and creates opportunities for communities, youth, and first-generation wealth builders.

The Founder
Jon Gladden
Founder and CEO, Gladden Global Network
U.S. Army Veteran Real Estate Professional Enterprise IT Engineer Maryland — DMV Area
jon@thegladdenglobal.com (410) 204-4070

Jon Gladden is a U.S. Army veteran, real estate professional, and enterprise IT engineer based in the DMV area. After years of working in high-level technical environments managing large-scale device deployment, automation, and Azure-based infrastructure, Jon recognized a parallel gap.

The same people who were never given access to technology at scale were also never given access to real estate wealth-building tools. GGN is Jon's answer to that gap.

Built with the discipline of a veteran and the systems mindset of an engineer, it is designed to be the platform he wishes had existed when he was starting out — one that connects the dots, educates every participant, and creates opportunities for communities, youth, and first-generation wealth builders.

We are not a traditional real estate company. We are a connection platform built to bring every participant in the real estate ecosystem together under one roof.

Our Mission

Growing Knowledge.
Creating Opportunity.

To grow the business knowledge of every participant in our network, create real opportunities across communities and youth, and give people — especially those who were never shown the door — a clear path to learn, connect, transact, and build generational wealth through real estate.
